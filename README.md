# Susitinkam dažniau

*"We should meet more often" — this time for real.*

An app that keeps the promise everyone makes and almost nobody keeps. You set a **private rhythm** for each person or group you love — how often you'd genuinely like to meet. The app watches the rhythms and helps you reach out before "kada nors" becomes never.

## How it works

1. **Add people & groups**, each with a private rhythm (weekly … twice a year) and tag. Nobody ever sees their tier.
2. The **rhythm ring** around each person fills as time runs out — green, amber, red.
3. When it's time, the app **drafts the invite** with real date options and a place. You share it as a normal message (WhatsApp, SMS, anything).
4. One tap confirms the reply — the plan lands in your calendar via **.ics export**, and the rhythm resets.
5. After you meet, jot a **private note** ("minėjo naują darbą") — it resurfaces next time you reach out.

## Privacy

Everything lives in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use *Settings → Export backup* to move your data between devices.

## Running it

It's a single static page — open `index.html`, or host the folder anywhere static (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages). Served over HTTPS it works offline and can be added to your phone's home screen as an app.
